### Hi, I'm Václav Ráš

Data engineer and analyst from Prague. I build the whole data path for companies: from tracking (GA4, Google Tag Manager, Consent Mode) through BigQuery data warehouses to recommender systems and AI agents that answer questions from company data.

I'm the founder of **[AnalyticsProof](https://analyticsproof.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=founder_profiles&utm_content=gh_readme_intro)**, a tool that replays your key user journeys every day and alerts you when tracking or cookie consent breaks.

#### What I work on

- **Tracking** for websites, e-shops, apps and HbbTV: GTM (incl. server-side), GA4, Consent Mode
- **Data warehouses** in BigQuery and Dataform: web, app and CRM data in one place, numbers that agree
- **Data products**: recommender systems used by hundreds of thousands of viewers, AI agents over company data running in the client's own Google Cloud
- **Reporting and analysis**: Looker Studio, customer segmentation, Python

At Česká televize (since 2021) I built the BigQuery data warehouse for iVysílání and tracking across web, HbbTV and Smart TV, and I'm developing its recommender system and an internal AI agent over the data.
